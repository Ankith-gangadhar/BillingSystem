# 🛒 CASHIER QUICK GUIDE — MANGALORE STORE POS
### (For Mother and Store Staff — Keep by the Counter)

---

## 🟢 STEP 1: OPEN YOUR SHIFT (Morning)
1. Tap your name tile on screen (**Raju** or **Mother**).
2. Enter your 4-digit PIN (`0000` or `1111`).
3. Count the notes and coins in the cash drawer.
4. Type the opening amount (e.g. `1000`) and tap **"Open Shift & Start Billing"**.

---

## 🟢 STEP 2: BILLING PRODUCTS (3 Easy Ways)

### Option A: Barcode Scanner
- Point your barcode scanner at the packet barcode.
- It **beeps** and adds the item to the bill immediately.

### Option B: Search Bar (Top of Screen)
- Type 1 or 2 letters in the search bar (e.g. type `ko` for **Kori Rotti** or `ba` for **Banana Chips**).
- Press **Enter** on keyboard or tap the item in the list.

### Option C: Quick Touch Squares
- Tap any product square button on the screen to add 1 packet.

---

## 🟢 STEP 3: CHANGE QUANTITY OR GIVE DISCOUNT

- **Change Quantity**: Tap the **`+`** or **`-`** buttons on the item line.
- **Set Final / Discounted Price**:
  1. Click **Discount (F8)**.
  2. Type the final negotiated total (e.g. if total is ₹85, type `80`).
  3. Click **Apply Discount**.

---

## 🟢 STEP 4: ITEM NOT IN COMPUTER? (Custom Item)
If an item is new and not in the computer:
1. Tap the **"+ Custom (F4)"** button at top right.
2. Type the name (e.g. `Fresh Coconut Jaggery`) and the selling price.
3. Click **Add to Bill**.

---

## 🟢 STEP 5: FINISH SALE & COLLECT MONEY
1. Click the big green **"Complete Sale (F9)"** button at the bottom.
2. Choose **Cash** or **UPI**:
   - **Cash**: Type amount received (e.g. ₹500 note). The screen tells you exact **Change to Return**.
   - **UPI**: Show QR code to customer on screen.
3. Tap **"Finish & Print Receipt"**.

---

## 🟢 STEP 6: TAKING CASH OUT FOR SHOP (Milk / Auto / Tea)
1. Go to **"My Shift"** tab.
2. Click **"Record Drawer Expense"**.
3. Type amount (e.g. `60`) and reason (`Milk for tea`).
4. Click **Log Cash Event** so your drawer cash matches at shift end!

---

## 🟢 STEP 7: CLOSE SHIFT (End of Work)
1. Go to **"My Shift"** tab.
2. Click **"Close Shift"**.
3. Count all physical cash in the drawer and type the total number.
4. Tap **"End Shift & Log Count"**.
